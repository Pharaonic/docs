## How do I install a package?

Simply run `composer require pharaonic/package-name` in your Laravel project. Check the documentation for specific package instructions.

## How quickly will you reply?

Pharaonic is maintained by a small team, so there's no guaranteed response time. GitHub issues and Discussions are where questions get answered and tracked; Discord is best for a quick question.

## Can I request a custom package?

Yes! We offer custom development services. Reach out on [Discord](https://discord.gg/XQG9RhvEvf) describing your requirements, and we'll get back to you with a quote.

## Where can I report bugs?

You can report bugs directly on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/pharaonic). Not sure it's a bug? Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/Pharaonic/discussions) or let us know on [Discord](https://discord.gg/XQG9RhvEvf).
